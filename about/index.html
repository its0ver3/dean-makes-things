How I got here

When I was a kid, I thought I was going to make video games. I spent all my free time in 3DS Max, ZBrush, and Photoshop. For some unknown reasons, I drifted from that path. I ended up doing minor work in film, working retail, and eventually working in healthcare.

After taking a break to work in sales & delivery operations at Tesla, I did some teaching and then moved on from healthcare completely. I’ve returned to the childhood joy of making things for fun on the computer. I hope to use these amazing (and scary) AI tools to carve a unique path forward for my career.
