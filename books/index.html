Books

A few books I keep recommending:

  • The Beginning of Infinity — David Deutsch
    Changed how I think about progress and good explanations.
  • Working Backwards — Colin Bryar and Bill Carr
    The clearest book I know on practical product discipline.
  • The Creative Act — Rick Rubin
    Less a manual than a permission slip to pay attention.
  • The Death and Life of Great American Cities — Jane Jacobs
    A masterclass in noticing what is actually happening.
