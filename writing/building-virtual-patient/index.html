Building Virtual Patient - an RMT exam prep tool

August 16, 2026

I started building this when I was teaching at the RMT college in Vancouver. In class we had to go over mock patient scenarios where I would be the patient and the students would ask me questions. The engagement wasn’t super strong, and it was usually the same students asking questions. I thought it would be more fun if we could interview a patient together and talk about why we would ask certain questions.

So I built a simple web app hooked up to a Claude API and gave it a patient scenario. It only took a bit of back and forth with Claude Code to get something better than the responses I would give in class. It felt like we were talking to a “real” patient.

I trialed it with one of my classes, and they were blown away. I tried it with another class and got the same response. So in my spare time, I kept chipping away at it and evolving the product.

Students started asking if they could use it to study, and I thought, why not? So I added auth, improved the UI, and added specific cases that my exams would have. I had almost 50 users in the first few weeks.

I’ve continued to work on it, but since leaving the profession, it’s lost some of its luster. My plan was to turn it into a board-exam-specific prep tool. It would help students gain confidence for the exam, and there’s a (small) market for it. People pay hundreds of dollars for a one-time, in-person prep exam; I could see them spending half that for unlimited virtual prep.

Regardless of what comes of it in the future, I learned a ton from this project.
